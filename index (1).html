Method

How I found clients nobody else was pitching

The target was narrow on purpose: small businesses with at most 10 employees, in an English-speaking country, with no ads running.

  1. Find

    Browse Instagram and Google Maps for clinics that match the profile and show no active ads.

  2. Reach out

    Start with email and social DMs, add text messages, and finally cold calls.

  3. Sell on Zoom

    About a year of outreach produced 7 sales calls.

  4. Launch

    Four clients closed. I built and managed their lead campaigns on Meta.
